About my name

My legal name is Zijun Zhuang. In Chinese it is written 庄梓俊, and my family chose each character with care.

庄
zhuāng · family name, as in Zhuangzi

My family name is shared with Zhuangzi, the great Daoist philosopher and thinker. My family hoped I would grow up with some of his wisdom.

梓
zǐ · catalpa tree

It carries the wood radical 木. In the Chinese five-element tradition my birth chart was short on wood, so this character was chosen to balance it.

俊
jùn · handsome, talented

My parents' hope for me. I am still working on it.

Most people call me Keven, a name I have used since kindergarten. It is spelled with two e's, and it is not a typo.
